Facility Startup Protocols

Standard operating procedures for the Klinton Hydroelectric Power Plant.

Introduction

Welcome to the official GPS operating manual. This documentation is designed to assist Junior Operators and Engineers in the safe management of hydroelectric assets. Failure to follow these steps may result in a GRID_CASCADE_FAILURE.

Pro Tip Always check the frequency meters before engaging the main breaker. Stability should be at exactly 60Hz.

Turbine Startup

To begin energy production, follow these steps in order:

  1. Open Penstock Valve A-1 to 15% flow.
  2. Wait for RPM Gauge to reach 300.
  3. Synchronize Phase 1 with the Main Grid.

Fault Indicators

If you see a flashing light on the Annunciator panel, refer to the specific fault code below.

Most common faults are caused by over-voltage or low lubricant pressure in Turbine 2.
